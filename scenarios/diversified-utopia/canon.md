# Canon facts

These are confirmed facts about the real world at the start of the game. They outrank the summer
2026 briefing wherever the two disagree, and every role treats them as true. The briefing was
compiled from search snippets and has gaps; this list fills them. Add to it as you learn more.

## Models already released (as of October 2026)
- **OpenAI: GPT-6 is out.** GPT-6-class models, including GPT-6 Astra, have been available to
  users since early September 2026. GPT-5.6 is the previous generation, not the current frontier.
- **Anthropic: Claude Opus 5.5 (22 September 2026) and Fable 5.1 / Mythos 5.1 (September 2026)** are
  the current public and restricted models.

## Reasonable expectations
- **Anthropic's release cadence is fast.** Mythos 5 shipped in June 2026 and 5.1 in September. A
  Mythos 6 / Fable 6 generation within the first half of 2027 is a reasonable expectation, not a
  fantasy, and so are matching next generations from OpenAI and Google DeepMind.

## How to use this list
- If a player names a model, product or fact that this list or the briefing does not cover, do not
  flatly deny it. Treat it as plausible if it fits the cadence and the world state, and reconcile it
  into the world state; reject it only when it clearly contradicts something established.
