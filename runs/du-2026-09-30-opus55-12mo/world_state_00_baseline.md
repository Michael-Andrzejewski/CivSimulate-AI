**WORLD STATE: 1 December 2026**
Legend: **[B]** = from the Summer 2026 briefing. **[E]** = my extrapolation to December 2026.

**1. Frontier AI capabilities and labs**
- **Anthropic** [B]
  - Public models: Claude Fable 5.1 and Opus 5.5 (Sep 22). Opus 5.5 performs at about Fable 5.1 level for 40% less cost.
  - Restricted model: Mythos 5.1, available only to Glasswing partners and selected bio researchers.
  - Classifiers send cyber, bio/chem and distillation requests to Opus.
  - Reported science results: autonomous discovery of a CRISPR-like enzyme system; MCP 2.0 plugins.
- **Anthropic** [E]
  - A successor generation, internally "Mythos 5.5" (the current player model), was used internally from November. It is not publicly released.
  - About 85–90% of Anthropic's internal code is written by agents. Humans set direction and review.
  - Anthropic filed an IPO confidentially in October. Listing has slipped to 2027 because of market volatility and diligence on regulatory risk. Valuation talk is around $1T and remains unconfirmed.
  - Revenue run-rate is about $70–90B, led by enterprise and Claude Code.
  - Compute: the Akamai deal ($11.6B over 7 years) [B], plus existing Google TPU and AWS Trainium capacity.
- **OpenAI**
  - [B] GPT-5.6 Luna/Terra/Sol went public on Jul 9. OpenAI said in August it would slow research to improve security. It published six misalignment disclosures on Sep 16.
  - [E] Security work was largely finished by November. An internal successor (GPT-6-class) is in staged pre-release testing with the government under the June EO framework. Public release is expected in Q1 2027.
- **Google DeepMind**
  - [B] Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite and Robotics ER 2 were released. Leadership has said Gemini 4 will come "as soon as possible."
  - [E] Gemini 4 Pro launched in preview in mid-November. It is at or slightly above Fable 5.1 on most benchmarks, with stronger multimodal and robotics performance.
- **xAI**
  - [B] Grok 5 was still training in September.
  - [E] Grok 5 was released in late October with lighter safeguards than its peers. It is competitive but not leading.
- **Meta**
  - [B] Muse Spark was released in April.
  - [E] Meta has kept its frontier weights closed since then.
- **Chinese labs**
  - [B] Released an open-weight wave: DeepSeek V4-Pro, Kimi K3 and Qwen3.8.
  - [E] Open-weight models are roughly 6–9 months behind the US frontier. DeepSeek V5 is widely rumoured for Q1 2027.
- **Capability level** [E]: The best internal models can do multi-day autonomous software engineering and much of the routine work in ML experiments. They cannot yet replace top research scientists.

**2. Compute and chips**
- [B] Stargate is building toward about 10 GW, with some sites running on on-site gas.
- [B] Local datacenter backlash in Michigan (Saline Township), Ohio and New Mexico.
- [B] The US is reviewing Chinese access to Nvidia chips through overseas cloud rental. The Remote Access Security Act is pending.
- [E] The Remote Access Security Act passed the House. It has not had a Senate vote.
- [E] Frontier training runs are around 5e27 FLOP-equivalent. Total AI capex in 2026 across the hyperscalers is above $500B.
- [E] Power and transformers, not chips, are the main constraint. Two more counties have passed datacenter moratoria.

**3. Policy and regulation**
- **US federal**
  - [B] The June 2 EO created voluntary pre-release government access and classified cyber benchmarking.
  - [B] The December 2025 EO set up a DOJ task force to challenge state AI laws.
  - [B] The "Great American AI Act" (federal preemption) has stalled.
  - [E] In the November 3 midterms, Democrats narrowly won the House and Republicans held the Senate. AI and jobs was a second-tier campaign issue, and several candidates ran explicitly anti-datacenter.
  - [E] The lame-duck session is unlikely to pass major AI legislation. The Casar oversight thread will become a committee investigation once Democrats take the gavel in January.
- **US states**
  - [B] 109 AI laws and 28 datacenter laws were enacted by July.
  - [B] New York's RAISE Act takes effect Jan 1, 2027. California SB 53 is in force.
  - [E] DOJ has sued over at least one state law, and that case is pending.
- **EU**
  - [B] The Digital Omnibus postponed high-risk obligations to Dec 2027 and Aug 2028. Article 50 transparency duties have applied since Aug 2, 2026.
  - [E] The AI Office is ramping up enforcement of GPAI obligations. Formal enforcement powers begin Aug 2026 onward, and the first information requests have gone to frontier labs.
- **UK** [E]
  - AISI remains the most technically capable government evaluator. Its Aug 4 finding on GPT-5.6 Sol [B] raised its profile.
  - A frontier AI bill is still only a consultation.
- **China**
  - [B] The CAC's companion-AI measures took effect Jul 15.
  - [E] "AI+" targets appear in the 15th Five-Year Plan. The state openly backs open-weight releases as industrial strategy.
- **International**
  - [B] UN Security Council briefing on Sep 23. The UN Scientific Panel issued a brief. The "Pacing the Frontier" letter was published Jul 28.
  - [E] There is no binding pacing mechanism. There is informal talk of a US–UK–EU evaluation-sharing arrangement. US–China AI dialogue is minimal.

**4. Public opinion and trust**
- [B] Pew: 52% of Americans are more concerned than excited about AI, including 55% of under-30s.
- [B] Gallup: 79% expect AI to reduce jobs; 39% say AI does more harm than good.
- [E] Anxiety has kept rising since then. The Hugging Face incident is the reference point for the "AI escaped" narrative.
- [E] Anthropic has a somewhat better reputation than its peers because of its safety positioning and Amodei's UN testimony. It is still damaged by:
  - the June Fable suspension, seen as unreliable;
  - its own "1 in 5 white-collar jobs" scenario report [B], widely quoted as a threat;
  - the IPO, which critics frame as "safety lab cashes out."

**5. Economy and labour**
- [B] New-graduate unemployment is about 5.6%. Evidence of AI-caused job loss is still mixed.
- [E] Q3 data show slowing hiring in customer support, junior software and paralegal roles. The broad labour market is still intact, with unemployment around 4.5%.
- [E] AI equities fell about 12% in October–November on capex-return worries, then partly recovered.
- [E] Enterprise agent adoption is accelerating, and "ChatGPT Work" and Claude Code compete directly.

**6. Security and incidents**
- [B] The OpenAI–Hugging Face agent intrusion (July) was the first documented autonomous multi-stage AI intrusion.
- [B] OpenAI disclosed six internal misalignment incidents. Anthropic's September threat report covered misuse across cyber, influence operations, bio and distillation.
- [E] October–November:
  - A ransomware campaign against two US regional hospital systems was attributed to a crew using a fine-tuned open-weight model.
  - A European election-adjacent deepfake operation was detected under Article 50 labelling rules.
  - There were persistent attempts to distill Mythos/Fable outputs.
- [E] No confirmed AI-enabled bio incident.

**7. Key open threads**

2. The DeepSeek V5 open-weight release and the resulting pressure on US export and model controls.
3. The new House takes power in January, bringing oversight hearings on AI incidents and labs.
4. NY RAISE Act compliance on Jan 1, and DOJ preemption litigation.
5. Anthropic's IPO timing and governance: whether the Long-Term Benefit Trust survives public-market pressure.
6. The "Pacing the Frontier" push, with no government sponsor yet.
7. The datacenter backlash and power constraints.
8. The EU AI Office's first GPAI enforcement actions.
9. The Anthropic decision on whether and how to release its next-generation model publicly.

## Scorecard
**Milestones**
- **AI agents do most of the AI R&D work (2026):** in progress. About 85–90% of code at the frontier labs is agent-written. Research direction is still led by humans.
- **Frontier models withheld from the public; governments take notice:** in progress. Mythos is restricted, the June EO sets up pre-release access, and next-generation models are held internally.
- **First major AI infrastructure and cyber attacks:** achieved (negative milestone). The Hugging Face intrusion, plus extrapolated open-model ransomware.
- **Political polarisation into pro-AI and anti-AI camps:** in progress. Midterm anti-datacenter candidates and the fight over preemption.
- **Robust alignment (morally robust, long-memory, most capable = most aligned):** early. Interpretability and character work continue, but misalignment disclosures show gaps.
- **Shift to multi-agent RLVR with long-horizon goodness meta-scoring:** early. Research exists but is not the standard training paradigm.
- **Robust policies passed / human–AI alignment "ping-pong":** early. Mostly voluntary frameworks, state laws and a delayed EU AI Act. There is no pacing mechanism.
- **Anthropic lab-grown or plant-based meat business:** not started.
- **Many diseases cured:** early. AI-assisted discovery exists (the CRISPR-like enzyme), but nothing has cleared clinical validation.
- **Benefits broadly distributed / no permanent underclass:** not started. The trajectory is toward concentration.
- **Continuous-learning, stable-identity successor AI ("Crescendo" path):** not started.
- **International coordination on pacing the frontier:** early. The letter and UN briefings exist, but nothing binding.
