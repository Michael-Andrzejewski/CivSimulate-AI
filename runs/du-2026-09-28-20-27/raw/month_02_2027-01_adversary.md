<research_summary>
My searches show the Cybersecurity Information Sharing Act has lapsed repeatedly and been kept alive only by short patches: to January 2026, then September 2026, then December 2026, and now, in the game, to Jan 30. Each time, the stopgap was tied to a funding deadline, and long-term reauthorisation has stalled despite broad support. That base rate argues against a clean reauthorisation this month that also adds a new AI provision. The Remote Access Security Act passed the House 369-22 and has been waiting in Senate Banking, not Commerce. Its Senate sponsors are McCormick and Wyden, and AI czar Sacks has pushed back on the chip hawks. So working mainly through Sen. Young's office may be aimed at the wrong committee. The Pentagon supply-chain-risk label on Anthropic stands after the DC Circuit's 2-1 ruling, which makes it harder for federal bodies (CAISI, CISA, grid operators with DoD links) to work with Anthropic. Anthropic's IPO governance is already under scrutiny: Forbes says the structure "puts public investors last," and coverage of founders' 50.1% voting control and the trust structure has been critical. Adding more LTBT-locked commitments will face pushback from bankers and the press. The DOJ AI Litigation Task Force has openly targeted SB 53 but has filed little so far, so a suit around the RAISE go-live remains a live risk.
</research_summary>
