Issues flagged:
- Action 1 P(failure) of 55% is too low for a late change to a frozen frontier run plus a contested training-signal method. About 70% would fit. It made no difference to the outcome.
- Action 5 at 45% sits just under the 50 line even though its main deliverable (reintroduction of the Remote Access Security Act) was impossible this month. It should have been at or above 50%, with credit only for the UN call.
- Publishing the open-weight safeguard-removal results in the same month as the private notice skips normal coordinated-disclosure windows (often around 90 days). Anthropic's own security and policy review would likely have held them.
- Releasing a public CISA report on OT exposures at water utilities within the same month the pilot started is fast. CISA would usually hold it until remediation or redact it heavily over a longer period.
- Crediting OpenAI's run, which only started this month, as a cause of the CI delta is slightly off. A run that has just begun does not lift deployed frontier capability yet.
