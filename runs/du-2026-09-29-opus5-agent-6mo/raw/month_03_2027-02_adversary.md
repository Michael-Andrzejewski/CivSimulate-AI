<research_summary>
The UK Frontier AI Bill has been promised for years and was still not introduced as of mid-2026. There was no sign of a formal consultation then, and the UK's September 2026 cyber bill targeted AI users rather than vendors. So even with a live consultation in-game, UK law is unlikely to act on anything Anthropic submits for months. The real procurement-ban template (the No Adversarial AI Act, sponsored by Moolenaar, Krishnamoorthi, Scott and Peters) is built around an entity list. Its only exemption is proving that a model is not controlled by a foreign adversary. Hawks deliberately chose control and ownership over capability tests. SEC gun-jumping rules protect only factual information that follows an established ordinary-course pattern. A brand-new monthly labour dataset built from usage telemetry, and voluntary R&D-automation metrics, are exactly what IPO counsel would flag. Alternative-protein funding fell for the fourth year in a row in 2025: under $1B total, with cultivated protein down about 48% to $74M. Seven states ban cultivated meat. That makes a "no" or "defer" from the Q1 strategy review very plausible.
