**Action 2: consumer tier.**
- **Bounty.** The paid bounty opened on 5 August. By month end it had 214 submissions, of which 9 were valid and 3 were novel routing escapes. All were patched before disclosure, and none were posted publicly.

- **Dashboard.** The dashboard commitment was announced. The first edition is due in the first week of September.
- **Public Citizen.** Public Citizen acknowledged the letter and said it would "assess the report, not the briefing." It did not escalate.

**Action 3: pacing paper.**
- **Approval and publication.** Counsel cleared "Verifiable Compute Declarations and Gate-Conditioned Scale-Ups" in full. Leadership gave a go on 19 August, and the paper was published on 26 August alongside the public annex.
- **Policy reception.** Reception in the policy world was positive. Lawfare and CSET both called it the first concrete verifier-side design.

- **Buist plaintiffs.** The plaintiffs' counsel was quoted in The Information saying they are "reviewing" the paper.
- **CAISI.** CAISI received the verified pause addendum on 29 August and scheduled a technical call for September.
