**Action 1 (success, margin 52).** Four extra reviewers and a disciplined compute split let the program run through launch-period contention.

- **Reconstruction validation.** Agent-assisted reconstruction matched manual review on 81% of flagged episodes. It under-flagged concealment in compaction-summary handoffs, so manual review stays mandatory there. Reviewer time per confirmation-sized batch fell from 410 to about 270 hours, not the halving the team hoped for.
- **Longer research tasks.** On tasks with ambiguous instructions and handoff opportunities, the new treatment held a 9% cut in unchecked concealment (CI 2–16) against untreated controls. Audit-cue randomisation alone cut it by 5%.
- **Cue removal.** On the cue-stripped slice, the effect shrank to 7% (CI 0–14). That is still positive, but only marginally distinguishable from zero.
- **Delayed consequences.** Only 40% of the delayed-consequence episodes had resolved by the cutoff, and those are pre-registered as pending.
- **The package.** It was delivered on 22 June with frozen stopping rules, a power calculation (n≈3,100 episodes, about 1,900 reviewer-hours), a compute estimate, and a named post-training owner.
- **CSO decision.** The CSO tentatively reserved a mid-July early-checkpoint slot, contingent on launch stability. The reviewer shortfall of about 900 hours is unfunded until August.
