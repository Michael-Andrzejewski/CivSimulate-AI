<research_summary>
SEC gun-jumping rules and practitioner guidance say that while a company is "in registration" it can publish routine factual business information. Anything that looks like conditioning the market can lead the SEC to push back the effective date. That is a real risk for any new public program Anthropic starts before pricing, such as OSF pre-registrations, jobs-outcome methodologies or evaluation results. OpenSSF's lifecycle process requires maintainers from more than one organisation, with at least two affiliations to advance past Sandbox, and the TAC usually defers applicants with weak governance. That means the kit's acceptance depends on CIS and Health-ISAC actually naming staff as maintainers, not just on what Anthropic writes. The Johns Hopkins Center for Health Security, the most obvious biosafety reviewer, has taken more than $40M from Open Philanthropy. Critics can therefore frame a "no-veto independent reviewer" as effective-altruism insiders reviewing each other. Reporting on open weights shows safety-stripped Qwen variants spread quickly after release. Together with the Georgia precedent, this suggests a real chance of a Qwen 4 or V4.5 incident in April, and of the Commerce open-weight debate turning against defenders' shared indicators.
