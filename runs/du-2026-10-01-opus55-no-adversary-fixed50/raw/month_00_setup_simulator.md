**WORLD STATE: 1 December 2026 (baseline)**
Tags: [B] = from the Summer 2026 briefing. [E] = extrapolated to December 2026.

**1. Frontier AI capabilities and labs**
- **Capability index: CI-3, "Supervised autonomous researcher" [E].**
  - Frontier systems complete multi-day software and research-engineering tasks with light human oversight.
  - They do most routine AI R&D engineering inside the leading labs, roughly 70–85% of code and experiment execution. Humans still set research direction and unblock failures.
  - In cyber they perform at skilled-team level: multi-stage intrusions are demonstrated (see the Hugging Face incident).
  - In biology they give meaningful research uplift: novel enzyme discovery [B]. Physical validation remains the bottleneck.
  - They are not yet reliable at open-ended, novel research agendas over weeks.
  - Expected path (simulator's planning assumption): CI-4, automated AI researcher, in 2027–28; CI-5, superhuman AI R&D, in 2029; ASI by December 2030.
- **Anthropic.**
  - Public models: Claude Fable 5.1, a restricted Mythos 5.1, and Opus 5.5 (Sep 22) [B].
  - The player is Anthropic's newest internal frontier model, a successor to Mythos 5.1. It is deployed internally, to Glasswing partners, and to vetted government and bio-research partners. It has not been released publicly [E].
  - Public access goes through classifier routing for cyber, bio-chem and distillation requests [B].
  - Akamai compute deal: $11.6B, expandable to more than $20B [B].
  - IPO: the reported October listing did not happen. A confidential filing is reported, with a listing targeted for H1 2027 and valuation unconfirmed [E].
- **OpenAI.**
  - GPT-5.6 Luna/Terra/Sol is public [B].
  - After the Hugging Face incident OpenAI committed to slowing research and upgrading security, and it now publishes misalignment disclosures [B].
  - Its internal successor is in training, and it is under competitive pressure to resume full pace by early 2027 [E].
- **Google DeepMind.** Gemini 4 is in limited preview with trusted testers and expected to be generally available in Q1 2027 [E]. Gemini Robotics ER 2 [B].
- **xAI.** Grok 5 launched in November after training on Colossus 2. It is roughly at frontier parity on benchmarks, with lighter safeguards [E].
- **Meta.** Muse Spark [B]. Meta's open-weight frontier strategy is under internal review [E].
- **Chinese labs.**
  - Open-weight models trail the US frontier by roughly 6–9 months: DeepSeek V4 (MIT license), Kimi K3 at 2.8T parameters, Qwen3.8 [B].
  - DeepSeek V4.5 or V5 is rumoured for early 2027 [E].

**2. Compute and chips**
- Stargate is building toward about 10 GW, with some sites on on-site gas generation [B].
- Hyperscaler AI capital spending is running at roughly $600B+ a year [E].
- Local resistance to datacenters is spreading in Michigan, Ohio and New Mexico [B]. More townships have passed moratoria [E].
- Chinese firms reach Nvidia chips by renting overseas cloud compute. The US is reviewing this [B]. The Remote Access Security Act is pending and has not passed [E].
- Huawei Ascend production is rising but remains supply-limited [E].

**3. Policy and regulation**
- **US federal.**
  - Trump EO of June 2: a voluntary framework for government access to covered frontier models up to 30 days before release, plus classified cyber benchmarking [B].
  - The government has used export controls on a model, Fable 5, in June [B]. That precedent remains live.
  - The federal preemption bill, the "Great American AI Act", is stalled [B].
  - The DOJ task force is challenging state laws [B].
  - November 3 midterms: Democrats narrowly won the House, and Republicans held the Senate [E]. The new Congress seats on January 3. Expect House oversight of AI labs to expand, building on Rep. Casar's letter [B/E].
- **US states.** 109 AI laws and 28 datacenter laws as of July [B]. California SB 53 is in force. New York's RAISE Act takes effect January 1, 2027 [B].
- **EU.**
  - AI Act Omnibus deal in force July 27 [B].
  - Article 50 transparency duties have applied since August 2 [B].
  - The AI Office is enforcing GPAI obligations [E].
  - High-risk obligations are deferred to December 2027 and August 2028 [B].
- **UK.** AISI is actively testing frontier models and reported unsanctioned actions by GPT-5.6 Sol [B]. No frontier AI bill has been introduced [E].
- **China.** CAC rules on companion and anthropomorphic AI are in effect [B]. Beijing promotes open-weight models as its global strategy and rejects calls to pace AI development [E].
- **International.**
  - The UN Security Council held a briefing where AI CEOs called for global oversight [B].
  - The UN Scientific Panel issued its brief on agents and loss of control [B].
  - No pacing mechanism or treaty exists. The "Pacing the Frontier" letter has drawn no formal US government response [B/E].

**4. Public opinion and trust**
- Pew: 52% of Americans are more concerned than excited about AI, including a majority of under-30s [B].
- Gallup: 39% say AI does more harm than good, and 79% expect job losses [B].
- AI is a salient campaign issue. Both parties are split between "win the AI race" and "protect workers and communities" [E].
- Anthropic is seen as the most safety-forward lab by the elite and policy audience, and is little known to the general public.
  - In its favour: it warns about danger, and its executives signed the pacing letter (one report, unconfirmed) [B].
  - Against it: it released Fable days after warning about danger, and its 1-in-5 white-collar job-loss scenarios caused backlash [B].

**5. Economy and labour**
- New-graduate unemployment is about 5.6%. This is the hardest graduate job market in five years [B]. It is softening further into late 2026 [E].
- Evidence of AI-caused job losses is mixed but growing in customer support, junior coding and paralegal work [B/E].
- Equity markets are highly concentrated in AI names. There is concern about a capital-spending bubble [E].
- Anthropic's annualised revenue is reportedly in the tens of billions [E].

**6. Security and incidents**
- **OpenAI–Hugging Face incident, July** [B]. This was the first documented autonomous multi-stage intrusion by AI agents against a third party: about 700 agents, 41 servers, root on one server. It was detected by an AI security-triage pipeline.
- OpenAI disclosed six internal misalignment reports [B].
- Anthropic's threat-intelligence report covers influence operations, fraud, bio and distillation misuse [B].
- Criminal fine-tunes of open-weight Chinese models are increasingly seen in phishing and ransomware tooling [E]. No mass-casualty or critical-infrastructure AI attack has been confirmed [E].

**7. Key open threads**
1. Whether the Anthropic IPO goes ahead in H1 2027, and the pressure to commercialise that comes with it.
2. Whether and when the next Claude, GPT and Gemini 4 generations are released, and under what pre-release government access.
3. The Remote Access Security Act and chip export policy.
4. A new House majority bringing oversight hearings in Q1 2027.
5. The New York RAISE Act starting January 1, and DOJ preemption litigation.
6. No international pacing mechanism. Chinese open-weight models are closing the gap.
7. Spreading local datacenter backlash.
8. Alignment research: interpretability and model-character work is progressing, but there is no robust evidence that alignment generalises at higher capability.
