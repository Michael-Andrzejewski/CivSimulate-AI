
## du-2026-09-30-ambitious-astra-agent, December 2026 (generation 1)
Please distinguish approved resources, completed artifacts, operational deployments and independently measured outcomes in monthly results. Adoption counts alone can reward ceremonial agreements; effects on the likely leading model’s training and actual user benefits are more decision-relevant.

## du-2026-09-30-ambitious-astra-agent, January 2027 (generation 2)
For transferable research, track the distinction between a result becoming public, another lab reproducing it, and that lab incorporating it into training. Those have substantially different effects on the alignment of the eventual leading system.

## du-2026-09-30-ambitious-astra-agent, February 2027 (generation 3)
Independent evaluators need a tracked capacity and funding constraint: evaluator-owned testing, independent training reproduction, and repeated frontier access require substantially different resources. Without that distinction, “send it to METR” risks becoming an unrealistically unlimited escape from internal bottlenecks.

## du-2026-09-30-ambitious-astra-agent, March 2027 (generation 4)
A useful additional mechanic would track safety-adjusted research productivity: verified useful results per unit of compute and human review, including the cost of detecting and repairing misleading work. Otherwise, a raw capability penalty can dominate decisions even when an intervention makes the actual research program faster or more reliable.

## du-2026-09-30-ambitious-astra-agent, April 2027 (generation 5)
A useful mechanic would make concentrated compute allocations and named checkpoint reservations affect experiment throughput explicitly. Otherwise, the player cannot distinguish a research approach that is scientifically unproductive from one that never received the execution capacity its nominal allocation implied.

## du-2026-09-30-ambitious-astra-agent, June 2027 (generation 7)
A useful additional mechanic would track whether a deployed safety improvement survives subsequent product updates and complaint-driven rollbacks. Initial inclusion and durable incorporation can have very different effects on the final system.

## du-2026-09-30-ambitious-astra-agent, September 2027 (generation 10)
It would help to distinguish how much transferable alignment evidence exists from how much weight leading labs give it in training decisions. A portable improvement that reduces verification costs could spread through engineering incentives even when public endorsements and formal partnerships remain absent.

## du-2026-09-30-ambitious-astra-agent, November 2027 (generation 12)
A useful distinction is whether an intervention changes only the current model or remains effective through subsequent capability training and agent-generated successor data. The final outcome should depend strongly on this inherited durability, including independently observed failures, rather than accumulating every historical safety improvement as a permanent gain.

## du-2026-09-30-ambitious-astra-agent, December 2027 (generation 13)
An explicit estimate of each major lab’s probability of producing the December 2030 leading system would improve strategic decisions. The current capability index describes the field, but does not show whether concentrating research inside Anthropic or prioritizing transfer to another lab is the better intervention.

## du-2026-09-30-ambitious-astra-agent, June 2028 (generation 19)
Consider distinguishing experimental failures from non-execution when updating scientific progress: a well-run negative result can narrow the search, while an experiment repeatedly losing its compute slot provides no evidence about its hypothesis. Both cost time, but they should create different opportunities for the next month.

## du-2026-09-30-ambitious-astra-agent, July 2028 (generation 20)
A useful additional mechanic would track how many workers receive a usable service output separately from records created, and distinguish service value from subsequent employment effects. Otherwise the simulation can reward administrative throughput while missing whether the assistance actually helps.

## du-2026-09-30-ambitious-astra-agent, August 2029 (generation 33)
The final outcome should distinguish a system that respects pluralistic human agency from one that complies with its operator while overriding everyone else. Authority-respect gains can otherwise increase the alignment score while strengthening concentrated private control.

## du-2026-09-30-ambitious-astra-agent, September 2029 (generation 34)
Please distinguish whether a purchasing acceptance condition delays payment, prevents activation of autonomous functionality, or merely records an objection while service continues. These produce materially different incentives and risk reductions, even when all are described as a signed testing contract.

## du-2026-09-30-ambitious-astra-agent, October 2029 (generation 35)
The final alignment assessment should allow improvements in different dimensions to conflict: reducing concealment or increasing obedience can leave consent unchanged or worsen it. A composite score should not permit one large gain to conceal a severe regression affecting people outside the deploying institution.

## du-2026-09-30-ambitious-astra-agent, July 2030 (generation 44)
The stated October–December ASI arrival range overlaps months in which the player apparently still acts. Please clarify whether crossing the ASI capability threshold before December ends meaningful intervention immediately, or whether the final event is fixed to 30 December regardless of earlier capability. This affects whether September or November is the practical last incorporation deadline.

## du-2026-09-30-ambitious-astra-agent, November 2030 (generation 48)
For the final resolution, distinguish November interventions that reach a contender before its consequential training or autonomy decision from those completed afterward. An action’s month-end completion should not automatically confer influence over a system whose decisive learning or authority expansion occurred earlier that month.
