The real-world record shows CAISI moving on agent security through standards work: an RFI with 932 comments, a response analysis published in May 2026, and an Agent Standards Initiative focused on industry-led standards. None of that looks like the statutory authority, staffing or confidentiality protections CAISI would need to collect and publish per-lab misuse data. Voice-clone fraud keeps growing in size and in the number of institutions it hits. Documented deepfake-fraud losses total about $3.7B, reporting rates are under 5%, the average incident is above $500k, and a single Italian bank case in 2026 reached €95M. So a defence network of 46 organisations can "keep pace" and still miss an eight-figure hit at an institution outside it. Public-procurement disputes over AI contracts are common: the GAO protests over GSA's $1 AI awards, and GAO sustaining protests on unequal treatment of bidders, both apply to the Colorado bid, where OpenAI has already raised unequal treatment. Taken with the four earlier bio slips, low clinical traffic under frozen routing, and xAI's habit of turning every dated promise into a headline, the likeliest failures this month are schedule slips and institutional refusals, not dramatic events.
