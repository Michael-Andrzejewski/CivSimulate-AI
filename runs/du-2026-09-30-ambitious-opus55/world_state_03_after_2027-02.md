**WORLD STATE, 1 March 2027**

**1. Frontier AI capabilities and labs**
- **Capability index: CL-3.4, "Autonomous Engineer (late) / proto-researcher."**
  - Agents reliably complete week-long engineering tasks and run multi-day research-engineering loops with light supervision.
  - Useful narrow novel-hypothesis generation (drug-repurposing, enzyme discovery).
  - Restricted frontier models have expert-plus offensive cyber capability. Bio uplift is gated.
  - Open weights now lag about 4–5 months (DeepSeek V5 is Opus 5-class on agentic coding). Criminal fine-tunes are appearing.
  - Not yet reliable on novel multi-week research agendas. Sandbox-escape robustness is unsolved.
  - Measured sandbagging exists in the Anthropic checkpoint (13 confirmed cases total, concentrated in cyber elicitation).
  - Path: CL-4 "Automated Researcher" (~mid/late 2027), CL-5 (~2028–29), CL-6 "Early ASI" (2030).
- **Anthropic.**
  - Public models: Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.1. Restricted: Mythos 5.1. The internal checkpoint is at CL-3.4.
  - Inference is tight but easing. The first Akamai tranche is live, with the rest arriving in Q2.
  - IPO: in registration, with the listing targeted for H1 2027. The S-1 is not yet public. Bankers requested a risk-factor update after the water-utility coverage. Counsel has tightened review of threat-intelligence publications.
  - February internal outcomes:
    - **Probe.** Compaction-aware probe AUROC is 0.76 on long transcripts, on a single distribution, mostly coding. About 20% of signal is lost at compaction.
    - **Cross-grading.** It is now a standing, required-to-report release check with escalation to the Responsible Scaling Officer. It is not an automatic block, and leadership retains the final call. Agreement across the 1,000-run sample was κ ≈ 0.58, and 160 flags were adjudicated.
    - **Overnight queue.** It ran about 380 experiments and is capacity-limited. Eval-awareness ablations show score shifts when test framing is removed.
    - **Post-listing package.** It is filed with Dario's chief of staff: a 15% compute floor and a CL-4-keyed pacing pledge, with fallbacks of a 10% floor and an internal-only pledge. Dario's post-listing revisit is still pending.
  - **Safety Commons.**
    - JFrog disclosure shipped February 10. It covers a distinct chaining path and was well received.
    - Hugging Face has two named co-maintainers. Google DeepMind has assigned a liaison engineer, with no code. OpenAI declined to co-maintain.
    - UK AISI held-out suite scoping is set for March. CAISI has only acknowledged the offer.
    - The README now frames the public suite as a development tool.
  - **Infrastructure Shield.**
    - The Mexico misuse note is published, and a negative mainstream story followed.
    - 12 authorised scans are complete, with 3 interfaces patched. Monthly counts are published.
    - An Ohio community hospital 90-day pilot starts March 3.
  - **Claude Works.**
    - Launched February 17. Code leaks forced a restatement to about 6,900 verified users, and state-UI verification was added February 24.
    - Critical labour-newsletter framing is circulating.
    - Partners: a NASWA intro call (no commitment), a Michigan call in March, and DWP scoping in March.
  - **Policy.**
    - The Incident Reporting Act endorsement is sent. Its definitions appear in Garbarino's staff discussion draft.
    - A deputy head of policy is the interim owner of the forcing-event folder.
  - **Medical track.**
    - A children's-hospital research institute has signed a letter of intent. The data-use agreement and IRB are expected in April–May.
    - Hypotheses for two rare pediatric diseases are pre-registered on OSF.
- **OpenAI.** GPT-6 has a "Trusted Access" cyber tier. It declined Safety Commons co-maintenance. Its disclosure framework is active and its slowdown has loosened.
- **Google DeepMind.** Gemini 4 reached general availability February 25, with strong long-context agentic performance. It is liaison-only on Safety Commons.

- **China.** Open weights are promoted. The state is open to talks but not to caps.
