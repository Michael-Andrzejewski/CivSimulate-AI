<research_summary>
The IRS self-dealing rules (IRC 4941) and the private-benefit doctrine put real limits on a company-created charitable trust whose beneficiaries overlap with a donor's commercial interests. They also require that "charitable class" eligibility be defined broadly. A trust restructure is therefore a months-long legal project, not something that can be done in February. State attorneys general have already taken coordinated action against chatbots that give sycophantic or unlicensed advice, including a 42-AG letter and a Pennsylvania lawsuit. An AI tool that navigates unemployment benefits during primary season is exactly the kind of product that invites a "wrong advice" story or an AG inquiry. Critics of Anthropic's Economic Index have already said its reported exposure runs systematically above observed exposure and that physical occupations are under-represented. A monthly occupation-exposure report in an election year becomes ammunition for both campaigns, not a neutral data source. The DOL's AI Literacy Framework and the NAWB–Microsoft partnership show that incumbent vendors already hold the workforce-agency channel. Washington ESD's in-flight evaluation also has a firewall that a new product offer could appear to breach. Taken together, this month's biggest risks are institutional friction and optics, not technical failure.
