<research_summary>
Carrier network APIs that detect call forwarding already exist: the CAMARA Call Forwarding Signal API through GSMA Open Gateway, and vendors like XConnect. So FS-ISAC's carrier request is realistic, but carriers can simply point members to paid commercial products instead of adopting a free attestation standard. Small credit unions have to integrate through core processors, which is slow. Guidance on non-inferiority trials from the FDA and EMA stresses that the margin must be pre-specified and justified. That makes a result that is inconclusive (the confidence interval crosses the margin) a real third outcome that Action 1's two-way rule doesn't cover. Security writing from 2026 treats the evaluation harness itself as attack surface after the OpenAI–Hugging Face breach, so releasing parts of Anthropic's harness and its classifier-evaluation method will face information-hazard criticism. Meanwhile, the open-weight coalition and the administration's "moat" critique mean even a free release can be read as a compliance kit for licensing. The recurring pattern from prior months still holds: third parties (hospitals, DTMB, the Trust, the court, FS-ISAC) control the dates, so Anthropic's plans that depend on timing keep slipping.
