Voice-clone fraud against banks is well documented and growing fast. The ABA calls it a top threat, 91% of US banks are rethinking voice biometrics, and one AI voice-clone case drained €95M from Fideuram. DeepSeek V5.5's open low-latency voice forks make a big fraud incident in June both plausible and attributable. Michigan's moratoria have snowballed: 50+ municipalities by mid-2026 and state moratorium bills. Reporters are asking whether moratoria will hold up, which means pre-session company outreach will be read as positioning. Campus AI procurement is contested, and some institutions switched vendors in 2026, so vendor-run "fixes" meet sceptical, fast-moving buyers. For the board item, published work on third-party weight access (RAND, RUSI) stresses that access by outsiders adds real exfiltration risk. A security chair can use that to push the escrow question well past June. Overall, June's risks are mostly procedural: a slow court, a split House, and campus renewals that come due July 1.
