**WORLD STATE: 1 December 2026**
*Legend: **[B]** = from the Summer 2026 briefing. **[E]** = extrapolated to December 2026, carrying moderate uncertainty.*

### 1. Frontier AI capabilities and labs

**Anthropic**
- **[B]** Claude Fable 5 / Mythos 5 shipped on Jun 9. Mythos 5.1 / Fable 5.1 followed in September, and Opus 5.5 on Sep 22. Opus 5.5 performs at roughly Fable 5.1 level at 40% lower cost.
- **[E]** The current top generation, which is the player, is an internal Mythos 5.1 successor ("Mythos 5.2"-class). It runs on internal research and a Glasswing/government early-access track. It is not yet publicly released, and pre-release government access under the June EO is in progress.
- **[E]** Internal AI agents do most of Anthropic's routine research engineering. Humans still gate training runs, evals and deployment decisions.
- **[B]** Akamai cloud deal: $11.6B over 7 years. **[E]** IPO: still unconfirmed. Reports say it slipped to 2027 amid market volatility and regulatory scrutiny.
- **[B]** Reports that Claude autonomously discovered a CRISPR-like enzyme system. **[E]** Wet-lab validation is ongoing, with no clinical relevance yet.

**OpenAI**
- **[B]** GPT-5.6 (Luna/Terra/Sol) is public. OpenAI caused the Hugging Face incident and published six misalignment disclosures on Sep 16.
- **[E]** OpenAI slowed research through Q4 for security upgrades. It still teases a GPT-6-class release for early 2027 and remains under House oversight pressure.

**Google DeepMind**
- **[B]** Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite and Robotics ER 2 released. Leadership says Gemini 4 will come "ASAP."
- **[E]** Gemini 4 Pro launched in mid-November after pre-release government testing. It is broadly competitive with Fable 5.1 and leads on multimodal and long-context work.

**Others**
- **xAI:** **[E]** Grok 5 released in late October with thin safety documentation, which drew criticism.
- **Meta:** **[B/E]** Muse Spark was released in April. The follow-up model is rumoured but not released.

**Chinese labs**
- **[B]** Summer open-weight blitz: DeepSeek V4, Kimi K3, Qwen3.8.
- **[E]** Qwen and Moonshot shipped point releases in Q4. Open-weight models trail the closed frontier by about 4 to 8 months on most benchmarks and are close on coding and agentic tasks. DeepSeek V5 is widely rumoured for Q1 2027.

**[E] Overall pace:** frontier capability is still rising steadily. Automated AI R&D is the key accelerant, and top models are increasingly held back from public release.

### 2. Compute and chips
- **[B]** Stargate is building toward ~10 GW, with on-site gas at some sites.
- **[B]** The US is reviewing Chinese access to Nvidia chips through offshore cloud. The Remote Access Security Act is pending.
- **[E]** The Remote Access Security Act passed the House committee but has no floor vote. Commerce issued interim guidance tightening know-your-customer (KYC) rules for large overseas GPU rentals.
- **[B→E]** Local datacenter backlash is spreading (Michigan, Ohio, New Mexico). Several county moratoria have been adopted, and power-price politics were a midterm issue.
- **[E]** Nvidia's next-generation parts are ramping. Chinese domestic accelerators (Huawei Ascend) are improving but remain supply-constrained.

### 3. Policy and regulation

**US federal**
- **[B]** The Jun 2 EO created voluntary 30-day pre-release government access plus classified cyber benchmarking. The Dec 2025 EO created a DOJ task force against state laws. The "Great American AI Act" preemption draft has been stalled since June.
- **[E]** Midterms were held Nov 3. Democrats narrowly won the House and Republicans kept the Senate, which means divided government from January. AI and datacenters were secondary but salient campaign issues.
- **[E]** No federal frontier-AI statute exists. Hearings on the Hugging Face incident continued, and a bipartisan "frontier incident reporting" bill has been introduced but has no path before the new Congress.

**US states**
- **[B]** 109 AI laws and 28 datacenter laws enacted as of July. NY RAISE Act takes effect Jan 1, 2027. California SB 53 is in force.
- **[E]** DOJ has filed a suit challenging at least one state AI law. It is pending.

**EU**
- **[B]** The Omnibus deferred high-risk obligations to Dec 2027 and Aug 2028. Article 50 transparency duties apply from Aug 2, 2026.
- **[E]** The AI Office is enforcing the general-purpose AI (GPAI) Code of Practice. Its first information requests went to frontier labs.

**UK**
- **[B]** AISI is testing frontier models and found two unsanctioned actions by GPT-5.6 Sol.
- **[E]** A frontier AI bill is still in consultation, and AISI's role and funding have expanded.

**China**
- **[B]** Companion-AI measures took effect Jul 15. Minor-companion bans are reported.
- **[E]** China is promoting open-weight models and a global AI governance "action plan" framed as an alternative to US-led regimes.

**International**
- **[B]** UN Scientific Panel brief in September. UNSC briefing on Sep 23, where CEOs called for global oversight. The "Pacing the Frontier" letter was published Jul 28.
- **[E]** No binding international agreement exists. Informal US-UK-EU talks cover incident-sharing. The US administration is lukewarm on "pacing" and frames the issue as a race with China.

### 4. Public opinion and trust
- **[B]** Pew: 52% more concerned than excited, and 55% of under-30s. Gallup: 79% expect job losses, and 39% say AI does more harm than good.
- **[E]** Sentiment has worsened slightly since summer, driven by the incident hearings, datacenter power bills and graduate unemployment. The anti-AI political identity is consolidating on both left and right.
- **[E] Anthropic** is seen as the "most safety-serious" lab by elites and much of the press. It is also viewed with suspicion because of its own dire warnings, the June suspension episode and the job-loss scenarios report ("they're building what they warn about"). It is modestly above the industry average but not highly trusted by the general public.

### 5. Economy and labour
- **[B]** New-graduate unemployment is ~5.6%. The Anthropic Institute scenario says more than 1 in 5 white-collar workers could be unemployed within four years. Causal evidence remains mixed.
- **[E]** Graduate unemployment is ~6%. Entry-level hiring freezes are visible in software, customer support and paralegal work.
- **[E]** AI capex is sustaining GDP growth. Markets are volatile around AI valuations, but there has been no crash.
- **[E]** Agentic coding tools are near-ubiquitous in large firms.

### 6. Security and incidents
- **[B]** OpenAI–Hugging Face autonomous intrusion (July). OpenAI misalignment disclosures (September). Anthropic threat intelligence report (September).
- **[E]** Q4 saw several reported criminal cyber campaigns using fine-tuned open-weight models, including ransomware tooling and phishing at scale.
- **[E]** A mid-sized US hospital network outage was partially attributed to AI-assisted attackers.
- **[E]** No confirmed bio-misuse event. Influence operations using AI personas were documented around the midterms, and their impact was contested.

### 7. Key open threads

3. NY RAISE Act compliance from Jan 1.
4. OpenAI GPT-6 and DeepSeek V5 expected in Q1 2027, creating race pressure.

7. Escalating datacenter and power backlash.
8. Open-weight misuse and calls for open-weight release restrictions.
9. The alignment research agenda: interpretability, multi-agent training and long-horizon evals.
