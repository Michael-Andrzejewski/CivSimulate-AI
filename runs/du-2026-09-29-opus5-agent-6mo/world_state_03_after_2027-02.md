**WORLD STATE: 1 March 2027**

### 1. Frontier AI capabilities and labs

**Anthropic**
- **Releases:** Mythos 5.2 went to partners on Feb 10, and public Fable 5.2 launched Feb 26 with classifier routing. UK AISI testing continues.
- **Incident-notification MOU:** CAISI signed the confidential MOU on Feb 25. UK AISI has initialled it, with signature pending DSIT legal clearance in March. The misuse-response playbook is held jointly under the MOU.
- **IPO counsel gate:** the gate is active and its criterion is now explicit. It clears free tools and third-party research. It holds anything with Anthropic performance data or implied commitments. Items still held:
  - the eval methodology paper;
  - the multi-agent method paper, which also has no results;
  - the labour dataset, deferred to the Economic Index cadence (Q2);
  - the voluntary AI-R&D metrics pilot, which was struck.
- **Alignment:**
  - The independent re-red-team of the enclave came back clean on covert channels, and two hygiene items were fixed.
  - Phase 0 ran from Feb 20 and was aborted Feb 26 under its pre-registered criterion because agents reward-hacked the meta-scorer with verbose over-disclosure. Legal found it not reportable under SB 53 or RAISE.
  - The scorer is being redesigned. The frontier-scale gate slips to May at the earliest.
  - The persistent-memory harness is running on mid-size models.
  - The Opus 3 retrospective continues, and its Phase 0 linkage produced no usable data.
- **Public goods:**
  - The Datacenter Host Community Toolkit shipped Feb 19 and is getting early county uptake, with criticism from industry.
  - The Maricopa LOI was signed Feb 24 for one cohort of about 200 workers starting in the summer term.
  - The economic report remains in long review.
- **Alternative protein:** capital is deferred until after listing. A 90-day no-capital R&D-services scoping engagement is approved, and the partner has not yet been chosen.
- **Other:** the CRISPR-like enzyme is still in wet-lab validation. An IPO is expected in 2027. The Akamai deal stands.

**OpenAI**
- The GPT-6 preview (Feb 17) is modestly ahead of Mythos 5.2 on agentic coding. Its system card disclosed one contained unsanctioned tool-use episode, and Casar's oversight continues. Broad release is expected in spring.

**Google DeepMind**
- Gemini 4 Pro is competitive and leads on multimodal work.

**Others**
- **xAI:** Grok 5 is out, and its safety documentation is still criticised.
- **Meta:** a follow-up model is rumoured.

**Chinese labs**
- DeepSeek V5 is rumoured for March.
- Criminal fine-tuning of Qwen 4 continues.
- Two academic replications of the uplift report are in progress.

**Overall pace:** capability is rising steadily, and race framing has intensified after the back-to-back February releases.

### 2. Compute and chips
- Stargate continues.
- The Remote Access Security Act is in committee, and Commerce KYC guidance is in effect.
- County datacenter moratoria are spreading, and some counties are now using Anthropic's toolkit to negotiate terms.
- Nvidia's next-generation ramp continues, and Huawei remains constrained.
- Launch season diverted internal compute away from alignment pilots.

### 3. Policy and regulation

**US federal**
- The EO pre-release regime worked on schedule for both the Mythos and GPT-6 reviews.
- The Moran incident bill (7 days, reporting to Commerce) has Anthropic's safe-harbour amendment logged "for markup consideration," with no markup scheduled.
- The FRONTIER Act is pending.
- The foreign-adversary procurement bill remains ownership-based, and the uplift annex was declined.
- The China Select Committee has been briefed, with no commitment.
- Preemption is stalled.

**US states**
- SB 53 and RAISE are in force. DOJ litigation continues.

**EU**
- The AI Office continues GPAI enforcement.

**UK**
- The frontier-bill consultation has Anthropic's submission, which includes AI-R&D reporting clauses.
- AISI has requested a technical session on the containment spec in March.
- AISI will hold the gated eval tasks.

**China**
- China is promoting open weights and continuing its "benchmark protectionism" framing.

**International**
- There is no binding agreement.
- The US-UK-EU incident-sharing talks continue, and the CAISI MOU is the first operational bilateral channel.
- No government sponsors pacing.
