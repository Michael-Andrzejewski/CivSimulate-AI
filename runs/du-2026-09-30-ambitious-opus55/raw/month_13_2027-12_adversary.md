Real pre-game sources back up the world state's capability path. On 7 September 2026 OpenAI said it had reached its "automated research intern" goal, reported 3.1 agent-workdays for every human workday, set a target of a fully automated AI researcher by March 2028, and admitted it "does not yet know how to safely get all the way to aligned, full RSI." The world state includes the target but not the underlying announcement, which is another case of the reconciliation problem I filed in December. Anthropic's real S-1 (filed 1 June 2026, reported 29 September 2026) lists about $518B in compute and cloud obligations and names the LTBT's control of the board as a possible "safety over profits" discount. Once public, that combination makes shareholder pressure on the December compute floor and on how disclosures land a concrete risk. DeepSeek's real release pattern is MIT-licensed weights on short notice (V4-Flash on 31 July, V4-Pro GA on 13 August), its architectures are new (mHC/MoE), and it has no third-party security audit. All three weaken a "day-zero safety pack" plan. WIOA Eligible Training Provider List (ETPL) listing is decided state by state and requires completion, placement and earnings data, so the Claude Works workforce-board route cannot pay off within one month. The EU AI Office is under-resourced and runs consultations over quarters. Overall, this month's main risks are self-inflicted: how the disclosure is framed, what 21-day unattended internal runs actually do, and whether the board finds the pre-set trigger too easy to fire.
