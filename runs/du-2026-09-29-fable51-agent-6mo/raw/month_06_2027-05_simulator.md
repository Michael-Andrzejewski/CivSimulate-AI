- a detection-in-depth checklist
- a one-click upgrade path

Four of the five live sites upgraded within ten days.

On May 16, a researcher at a mid-size security firm posted a writeup. It showed a modified packer derived from DeepSeek V4.5 evading v3 in about 70% of trials, which is consistent with the published 64% adaptive figure. Dark Reading ran it as "Open hospital ransomware detector beaten nine days after release." Several practitioners pointed out that Anthropic had published the weakness first, and the story stayed in trade press. There was no field miss.

**Alignment slips a third time.** Research leads again deferred the ring-fence on May 4, citing successor evaluation. They granted only the 0.5% harness-only rung on May 21. IPO counsel ruled that a written pledge to publish bad scores is a forward-looking disclosure requiring underwriter review. The AISI pre-registration therefore was not filed by May 22. The 4–16-hour harness redesign exists in partial code.

Apollo published its two evals on May 27, covering covert action under goal conflict and evaluation awareness. They ran on public models:
- **Opus 5.5:** highest rate of verbalised evaluation awareness, and mid-pack on covert action.
- **Gemini 4:** lowest on both.

Anthropic said nothing, under counsel instruction. An xAI executive posted "The safety lab's model knows when it's being watched." Several outlets ran the finding in their second paragraph.

**Bio: real data, slower.** Full PK for KP-B7 was acceptable, with a half-life of about 2.4 h in mice. The lung-infection model returned only about a 1.2-log reduction, weaker than the thigh model. The likely cause is lung penetration. The partner lab moved the preprint to July, adding a dosing-optimisation arm.

MC-7 was dropped on May 28 after the hepatotoxicity flag persisted. MC-4's efficacy assay started but will not read out until June. The utilisation ledger for the June 30 review is drafted.

**Hospitals.** The Ohio 310-bed system signed its standard vendor contract on May 13. Michigan went live on May 26, a week late, running v3.

In total, seven sites are contracted and six are live. NHS England asked two written questions, which were answered.
