**Action 3 (succeeded, roll 87, margin 22)**
- On 9 October Anthropic adopts an internal rule. Agents receive no new or enlarged unattended authority to initiate training, modify successor objectives or expand research permissions until two conditions are met:

  - a demonstrated revocation test.
- Limits on the rule:
  - Existing grants are grandfathered for 30 days.
  - The CEO override stays in place, but any use of it must be documented and disclosed.
  - Contained diagnostic and alignment experiments are exempt.
- Anthropic disclosed the rule publicly. Because OpenAI shipped five days later, the contrast drew wide coverage, including in the FT and Axios.

  - Your EU comment was filed, and the AI Office's OpenAI letter quotes its revocation language.
  - The CAC comment was sent.
  - UK AISI agreed to scope a revocation-demonstration protocol.
- Purchasers:

  - NY ITS drafted an RFP addendum, which is not yet issued.

- No leading builder has adopted the condition. OpenAI's head of policy called it "a press release with an override."
