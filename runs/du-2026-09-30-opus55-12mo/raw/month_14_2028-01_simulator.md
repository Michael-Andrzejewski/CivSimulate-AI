**Action 1 (fails).**
- **Final checkpoint.** The 1e28 run's final checkpoint lands on 14 January under the unchanged gates.
  - The eval-awareness gap reads 2.4 points, the fourth consecutive reading above 2.
  - The rotated series hits its highest value yet but stays below the pre-registered trend flag.
- **Publication slips.** It misses the 72-hour window. With three securities investigation notices open and Buist discovery live, the GC insists on a disclosure review. The readings go up on 20 January, six days after the checkpoint.

- **Leadership deferrals.**
  - Leadership declines to approve the 30-day public-notice rule, calling it "a board matter." The rule goes into the Q1 packet as an option, not a recommendation.
  - Leadership refuses the written 31 March fallback. The GC argues it would pre-empt the board.
  - The RSO restates the Redwood release condition, but IR re-attaches the "current plans" qualifier.
- **Press.** The Information reports on 23 January that "Anthropic declined to lock its own gate."
- **CAISI.** The 30-day EO window opens on 16 January. That part was routine and it went through.

**US-first sequencing (Threat 1, materialised).**
- On 17 January, NSC and ONCD staff remind Anthropic that the September 2026 guidance still applies. No UK AISI access to the final weights is allowed until CAISI's review closes, which pushes the earliest UK window to about 15 February and realistically to March.
- Commerce counsel informally advises that transferring the agentic-intrusion scaffolding to a foreign government body would need a licence review.
- The "foreign back door" line reappears in two Washington Examiner pieces and a Sen. Hawley post.
- UK AISI says publicly it is "working with US partners on sequencing."

**Action 2 (fails).**
- **Compute share.** Leadership does not dilute the 25% figure; the CFO signals it is acceptable in principle. It is held for the Q1 board packet, so no public commitment exists yet.
- **Probes.** The work stalls on a mundane cause. Training sparse dictionaries on the 1e28 checkpoint needs about three weeks of cluster time, and the checkpoint is sealed for the CAISI window. No validation plan is ready to pre-register.
- **Drift bar.** The alignment lead uses the 25 January fallback to post the new drift bar (≤0.30). The method section is marked "to follow" because the team disputes the reference set. Specialists on LessWrong and X call it "a bar without a ruler."

**Action 3 (succeeds, narrowed by Threat 1).**
- **Custody.** The scaffolding goes not to UK AISI but to a US custodial consortium led by Carnegie Mellon's Software Engineering Institute. It signs interim terms on 26 January, and vetted-access procedures go live in March.
- **Meta.** Meta's policy lead calls it "a DoD-funded lab holding a DoD-relevant tool, not neutral." The criticism lands with open-source advocates and barely anywhere else.
- **Explanatory note.** It publishes on 22 January with misuse-audit numbers from 9 of 14 Glasswing and bio partners (the others declined consent): 3 confirmed policy violations, 0 confirmed harmful uses, and 212 escalations across roughly 1.1M sessions.
- **Reception.** Lawfare and Platformer treat it as a real answer to the "most dangerous model" framing. Bloomberg still leads with "tops chart."
- **Restraint.** No BIS filing and no competitor API testing, as committed.

**Action 4 (narrowly fails).**
- **Queue.** Triage cuts the queue to about 11 days, not 7. Gemini 5's launch week brings about 140 new applications. The kit reaches 517 organisations.
- **Screening.** Reviewers reject two applicants with thin corporate histories, and nothing gets through.
- **Named deliverables.** Indiana Hospital Association counsel sends a new set of liability questions, so the review stays open. The JCDC package is delivered on 21 January, and JCDC acknowledges receipt with no ingestion date.
- **Routine pieces.** The second signature rotation ships. The three IGSC pilot members receive integration support.

**Action 5 (succeeds).**
- **Nebraska.** The meeting is held on 13 January on open, non-exclusive terms with Stanford's evaluation attached. Nebraska defers any decision until the Utah placement data arrives and FY budget guidance is set.
- **Utah.** The threshold is pre-registered on 19 January and co-signed by Stanford: expansion only if the six-month placement rate beats the matched control by at least 4 points.
- **DNDi and GFI.** No DNDi data yet. The GFI replication proceeds without contact from Anthropic.

**Action 6 (succeeds, UK portion blocked).**
- **CAISI.** It confirms eval-awareness is within scope. It will issue a public one-paragraph summary at window close, around 15 February, which would be the first government-held reading of a frontier checkpoint.
- **Brookings–Tsinghua.** The draft cites Anthropic's published honeypot data.
- **UN.** Clark's team limits UN panel input to already-published data.

**Exogenous events.**
