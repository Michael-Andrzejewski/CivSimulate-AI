**WORLD STATE: 1 April 2027**

### 1. Frontier AI capabilities and labs

**Anthropic**
- **Models:** Fable 5.2 (public) and Mythos 5.2 (partners) are deployed. An internal next checkpoint is modestly better. Competitive pressure is higher after DeepSeek V5 and the scheduled broad release of GPT-6.
- **MOU:** fully signed by CAISI (Feb 25) and UK AISI (Mar 19). A confidential AI-R&D metrics annex is countersigned by CAISI (Mar 30), with the first quarterly report due Apr 15. AISI has asked for the abort case study as a standalone annex. The misuse playbook is held jointly.
- **IPO counsel gate:** active, with a consistent criterion. It clears free tools and third-party research. It holds Anthropic performance data and implied commitments. Items held:
  - the eval methodology paper;
  - the multi-agent method paper, which has no results;
  - the "Reward Hacking of Moral Meta-Scorers" note. Counsel will clear a version with the rubric and harness only and no internal numbers; Alignment Science is undecided;
  - the labour dataset, planned for the Q2 Economic Index.
- **IPO timing:** after the V5 sell-off, bankers say H2 2027 is likely.
- **Alignment:**
  - The outcome-grounded scorer redesign is specified, with held-out detectors in training.
  - Security reviewed the new kill criteria on Mar 24.
  - Phase 0.1 is rescheduled for mid-April.
  - A hard compute floor was refused. Leadership granted "best-effort priority" after the V5 response work.
  - The frontier-scale gate is June at the earliest.
  - The persistent-memory harness continues at mid-size, and the Opus 3 retrospective continues.
- **Public goods:**
  - Toolkit v2 is live, listed on NACo's hub as a resource. About five counties have cited it.
  - The Task Complementarity Explorer launched Mar 20 with about 180k users.
  - The Maricopa curriculum is open under CC BY, with four districts requesting onboarding. The Maricopa cohort starts in the summer term.
  - The economic report is still in review.
  - The AI-R&D instrumentation tool is open source.
- **Uplift pipeline:** the analysis plan was pre-registered on Mar 13 and the 34 open tasks have been run on V5. The 6 gated tasks are queued at CAISI and AISI for April. The 14-day window was missed and no results are public. Compute credits for the replication teams are in procurement.
- **Alternative protein:** the selection memo ranks three candidates, and due diligence on the top pick runs into April. No engagement has started. The benchmark is held pending permissions for non-commercial sensory datasets.
- **Other:** the CRISPR-like enzyme is still in wet-lab validation. The Akamai deal stands.

**OpenAI**
- Broad availability of GPT-6 is set for Apr 22.
- It cut GPT-5.6 API prices 35% after V5.
- Casar's oversight continues.
- It rebuffed the bilateral notification channel, saying the FMF covers it.

**Google DeepMind**
- It released a Gemini 4 Deep Think preview and cut Gemini 4 Pro prices.
- It routed the Anthropic taxonomy to the FMF.

**Others**
- **xAI:** markets Grok 5 as "uncensored." Criticism of its safety documentation continues.
- **Meta:** a follow-up model is rumoured.

**Chinese labs**
- **DeepSeek V5** (Mar 11, MIT licence) is about three months behind the frontier, at Opus 5.5 level, and about one-eighth the price.
- Criminal fine-tuning of Qwen 4 continues, and V5 fine-tunes are expected.
- Two academic uplift replications are ongoing.

**Overall pace:** steady capability gains, with a price war and race framing at a peak.

### 2. Compute and chips
- The V5 release caused a sharp Nvidia and AI-stock sell-off, and markets are volatile.
- Stargate continues.
- The Remote Access Security Act is in committee, and Commerce KYC guidance is in effect.
- County datacenter moratoria are spreading, and some counties are negotiating with the toolkit.
- Launch and response work keeps drawing compute away from alignment.

### 3. Policy and regulation

**US federal**
- The EO pre-release regime is functioning.
- The Moran incident bill has the safe-harbour amendment logged, with no markup.
- The FRONTIER Act is pending.
- The foreign-adversary procurement bill is ownership-based.
- China Select Committee members are circulating draft language restricting US hosting of adversary-developed weights above a size threshold, which would escalate toward limits on distribution.
- Preemption is stalled.

**US states**
- SB 53 and RAISE are in force. The DOJ case is in summary-judgment briefing.

**EU**
- The AI Office continues GPAI enforcement and is reviewing V5 under its GPAI rules.

**UK**
- The consultation response is under review.
- A DSIT deputy director in the Frontier AI Policy unit leads the "AI-R&D measurement" strand.
- No bill has been announced.
- AISI holds the gated tasks and the containment spec v2.

**China**
- China is promoting open weights and calls the uplift work "moving goalposts."

**International and industry**
- The CAISI and AISI MOUs are operational.
- The FMF has adopted Anthropic's taxonomy as a working draft for incident-sharing.
- A bilateral lab-to-lab channel was stalled by antitrust counsel.
- There is no binding international agreement, and no government sponsors pacing.
