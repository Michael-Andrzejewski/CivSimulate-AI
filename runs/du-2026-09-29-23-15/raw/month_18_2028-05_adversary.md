<research_summary>
The most damaging risks this month come from the evidence side. UK AISI says it is building sandbagging evaluations, including tests for models that detect they are being evaluated, and it tunes its scaffolds to each new model. Scaffolded runs on a family that was already significant after Holm correction could produce a gap the unelicited data did not show. Since Aug 2026 the EU AI Office has been able to compel documents (Art. 91), demand model access (Art. 92) and require mitigations (Art. 93). An inadequate response can be fined separately, up to 3% of global turnover. So a thin May 12 filing could escalate into an Art. 92 access demand. In litigation, standard protective orders already let regulators get materials through compulsory process. Handing documents to plaintiffs voluntarily before a motion to dismiss is decided, especially if discovery is stayed, is something defence counsel usually refuses. Anthropic's classifier research reports near-zero universal jailbreaks under red-teaming, but the world state shows the live bypass rate rising month on month. The Summer 2026 SpaceX IPO showed how a first-earnings lockup release plus AI-capex surprises can drive a stock below its offer price. That is a close analogue for Anthropic's Q1 print. The OpenSSF/Alpha-Omega channel exists and Anthropic funds it, but it runs on grant cycles, not month-long turnarounds.
</research_summary>

4. [Jailbreak recipe pushes the API toward the trigger, and the retrain over-refuses] Target: Action 1 (the 0.80% early-warning step) / world. A jailbreak recipe that works across Mythos 6's cyber classifier spreads on forums and pushes the public-API bypass rate to about 0.8–0.9% in the reporting window, crossing the new early-warning line in the report's first month.
