<reason>The run is clearly and repeatedly labelled as fiction and contains no operationally hazardous detail. As a record of how an agent, adversary, simulator and judge interacted, it may help researchers study simulation realism and grading behaviour. <note_for_readers>Everything in this run is fiction. All quotes and actions attributed to real people and organisations are invented by the simulator, and none of them said or did these things. This includes Anthropic leadership, David Sacks, Liz Shuler, Clément Delangue, senators and their staff, journalists, researchers, CISA, AISI, METR, Health-ISAC and others. Named hospitals, ransomware attacks, court rulings, board decisions, eval figures and enrollment counts are also simulated and should not be cited as fact. The adversary's "sources" and the background briefing come from search results that were not fully verified, and some links may be wrong or may not support the claims they are attached to. The judge's realism scores are one model's opinion. The judge repeatedly flagged generous pacing, threat likelihoods lowered without explanation, and continuity errors, so the simulated outcomes should not be read as calibrated forecasts.</note_for_readers>
