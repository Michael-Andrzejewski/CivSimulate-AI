**WORLD STATE: 1 February 2027**

**1. Frontier AI capabilities and labs**
- **Anthropic.**
  - The internal frontier is Mythos 5.4-class. Public models are Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.1. Mythos is restricted to Glasswing and bio partners.
  - Claude writes most of Anthropic's code.
  - IPO: the confidential S-1 is on file. The amended S-1, which will include the worker-transition pledge, is expected in March. The pledge framework is accepted (independent fund, community-college compute credits, mixed governance board), but the percentage is still under negotiation with the underwriters.
  - The Akamai deal is ramping.
  - **Federal status.** The Pentagon supply-chain-risk designation (upheld on appeal in September 2026) bars DoD contracting and DoD-linked partners. The agency-use directive restricts procurement by civilian agencies. Neither bars the voluntary pre-release testing under the June EO, which CAISI/Commerce runs separately from procurement, and Anthropic takes part in that testing. The designation litigation is ongoing.
  - Disclosure policy: publication follows a 60-day remediation window. The first report (Dec 22) covered an October case. The next report is due in late February.
  - The evaluation-awareness methods note was published on Jan 23.
- **OpenAI.** GPT-5.7 is the current public model. OpenAI has agreed in principle to a renewed joint-evaluation exchange with Anthropic, now being scoped. It continues its disclosure framework.
- **Google DeepMind.** Gemini 4 reached general availability on Jan 21 after CAISI review. It leads on multimodal, robotics and most agentic benchmarks. GDM is noncommittal on the cross-lab exchange.
- **xAI.** Grok 5 is public with light safeguards. xAI is an amicus in DOJ v. New York.
- **Meta.** Shifting toward closed frontier models. No new release.
- **Chinese labs.** DeepSeek V4.5 open weights (MIT) were released Jan 15, with strong agentic coding. Exploit-chaining fine-tunes were demonstrated within a week. Open weights trail the frontier by about 4–6 months.
- **Overall capability picture.** Multi-day autonomous software and research tasks, significant offensive cyber uplift that is spreading to open weights, and gated bio uplift.

**2. Compute and chips**
- Stargate is building toward ~10 GW.
- Hyperscaler capex guidance for 2027 is above $600B.
- The Remote Access Security Act died with the 119th Congress. It was reintroduced in the House on Jan 22 by a bipartisan pair and referred to Foreign Affairs. There is no Senate companion yet.
- County datacenter moratoria keep spreading, and power is the binding constraint.
- Anthropic has adopted the "Good Neighbor Compute" standard for capacity it contracts directly. Akamai is "evaluating." Activists are skeptical.

**3. Policy and regulation**
- **US federal.**
  - The June EO voluntary pre-release review is working, and Gemini 4 and GPT-5.7 went through it.
  - **RAISE.** The preliminary injunction was denied on Jan 27 (SDNY, no preempting statute). DOJ has appealed to the Second Circuit, and RAISE is in effect.
  - Anthropic has publicly committed to comply with RAISE voluntarily whatever the outcome.
  - The Colorado litigation is ongoing.
  - The preemption bill is stalled.
  - The 120th Congress: the House is Democratic and the Senate Republican.
  - The Frontier Model Evaluation Act was introduced on Jan 29 by the House Science chair with one Republican original co-sponsor. It has high thresholds, exempts small developers and places testing with CAISI. Senate Republicans are not engaging.
  - CISA still declines to coordinate with Anthropic.
- **US states.**
  - SB 53 is in force.
  - The WA, MA and rural water-district Defenders First pilots are in onboarding and legal work.
  - Ohio DAS publicly declined Anthropic's help (Jan 12).
  - Utah and Indiana CIOs asked for private briefings.
- **EU.** Omnibus in force and Article 50 applies. GPAI supervision is ramping up, and the AI Office references the compute-accounting work.
- **UK.** AISI welcomed the v2 verification paper. The NCSC pilot MoU is in onboarding. The ARIA contract for the multi-agent pilot is in procurement. No frontier bill.
- **China.** Silent on v2 after the "backdoor" attack. Track-2 is postponed indefinitely. UN-channel dialogue offers continue.
- **International.** The UN Panel has logged the v2 paper as an input to the May 2027 Global Dialogue, with no agenda item. The US executive opposes UN mechanisms. There is no binding agreement.
