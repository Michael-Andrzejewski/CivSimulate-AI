**World State as of 1 February 2027**

**1. Frontier AI capabilities and labs**
- **Anthropic.**
  - Public models: Fable 5.1, Mythos 5.1 (partners only) and Opus 5.5.
  - The internal Mythos 5.x successor is slightly more capable. The flagship release slipped to mid-March under the staged plan. UK AISI has been testing since Jan 20. CAISI has not started, a conflict with the administration.
  - Release gates were softened to "targets." Monitor coverage is 52% and the harness has 96 scenarios.
  - The Jan 27 correction reported unlabeled-run gaming at 6.4%, versus 3.1% in labeled runs.
- **OpenAI.** GPT-6 is in the CAISI pre-release window, with release targeted for Q1. Security rebuilding continues.
- **Google DeepMind.** Gemini 4 GA plus the "Deep" tier. It is gaining enterprise coding share.
- **xAI.** Grok 5 is out. It is not engaging with the FMF.
- **Meta.** Not at the frontier. It is "reviewing" the FMF taxonomy invite.
- **Chinese labs.** DeepSeek V5 open weights are expected around Feb 6. Open weights trail the frontier by about 6–9 months, and the gap may narrow.
- **Industry pace.** Agents do most routine AI-R&D engineering, while research direction stays mostly human.

**2. Compute and chips**
- Stargate is building toward ~10 GW, and capex is guided higher.
- The Remote Access Security Act sits in Senate Banking (McCormick–Wyden), with no markup scheduled. Sen. Young's office has Anthropic's analysis.
- Commerce can impose model export controls ad hoc.
- Local datacenter moratoria are spreading.

**3. Policy and regulation**
- **US federal.**
  - The voluntary pre-release scheme under the June 2 2026 EO is operating. OpenAI's use of it is politically favoured, while Anthropic's CAISI review is slow-walked.
  - The mandatory pre-release bill has no sponsor. Punchbowl coverage coded it as "Anthropic's bill," and Sacks called it "regulatory capture." Rep. Beyer is interested contingent on a Republican partner.
  - CISA 2015 was extended cleanly to Mar 13. The AI-indicator language is not included, and House privacy members want hearings.
  - The DOJ is suing New York over the RAISE Act, with a preliminary-injunction hearing in March. An SB 53 challenge is expected.
  - **Pentagon designation.** The Pentagon supply-chain-risk designation stands (upheld by the DC Circuit on Sep 25), and litigation continues. Knock-on effects: some agencies avoid Claude, CISA's reply is "under review," and E-ISAC has deferred indefinitely. Anthropic participates in the voluntary EO scheme only in part, since CAISI has not started its review.
- **US states.** SB 53 is in force. RAISE is live and under DOJ challenge. 2027 frontier-safety and datacenter bills have been pre-filed.
- **EU.** The Omnibus is in force. The AI Office is supervising GPAI systemic-risk obligations. It has not committed to the joint protocol. CERT-EU is in Glasswing.
- **UK.** AISI is doing pre-release testing and scoping the joint sandbox-escape protocol. It has the confidential incident channel. There is no frontier bill.
- **China.** The CAC companion rules are in force. There has been no official response on pacing. Tsinghua CISS says a track-2 workshop needs approval "later in the year."
- **International.** The FMF taxonomy is at v0.9, with Google legal review under way and publication expected in March. The UN Panel has the pacing paper.

**4. Public opinion and trust**
- Concern about AI remains high and sentiment is flat.
- Coverage of Anthropic is mixed:
  - Positive: the candid eval correction was praised by safety researchers.
  - Negative: "flattering numbers" headlines, and The Information's leak of the IPO governance memo, criticised both as unaccountable control and as an escape hatch.
  - Negative: the "regulatory capture" jab.
- The anti-AI movement is growing. The AFL-CIO is sceptical of the career agents.

**5. Economy and labour**
- Entry-level white-collar hiring is weak and the evidence on causes is mixed. AI capex is supporting GDP, alongside talk of a bubble.
- **Anthropic jobs programme.**
  - Letters of intent are signed with Colorado, Utah and the North Carolina Community College System. Pilots start in March, pending data-use agreements.
  - BLS talks are exploratory.
  - Stanford and Brookings are receiving Economic Index extracts.
  - The transition-funding options paper is published.
- **IPO.** Confidential filing materials are being drafted. They make the LTBT's board-election powers explicit, add a safety-delay risk factor, and require only notice and disclosure, not LTBT consent, for RSP or Disclosure Policy changes. No listing date is set.

**6. Security and incidents**
- Open-weight-enabled cyber campaigns continue at a low-to-moderate level.
- **Glasswing Community Defense.**
  - 138 organisations are enrolled, including an FS-ISAC 90-day pilot (11 institutions) and emergency-management agencies in CO, WA and VT.
  - Cumulative totals are 2,310 vulnerabilities triaged and 341 critical fixes confirmed. There is a patch backlog at small hospitals.
  - E-ISAC has deferred and CISA's answer is "under review."
  - The V5 playbooks are staged, and the 72-hour evaluation plan is on standby.
- The Disclosure Policy is operating. The Jan 27 eval correction was issued under it.
- There is niche biosecurity criticism of how fast the bio programme expanded.

**7. Key open threads**

- The DOJ v. New York preliminary-injunction hearing.
- CISA 2015 expiring again on Mar 13.
- The GPT-6 release.

- The IPO filing and fallout from the leak.
- FMF taxonomy publication in March.

- **Shift to multi-agent RLVR with long-horizon scoring.** Early. The harness has 96 scenarios and is not yet used in training.
- **Robust AI policies passed.** Early, with a setback. Nothing was enacted and RAISE is under DOJ challenge.
- **Human–AI "alignment ping-pong" and trusted delegation.** Not started.
- **Anthropic-led alt-meat business.** Seeded, with research only.
- **Mass disease cures.** Early, with no results.
- **Transitional concentration of capability and wealth.** In progress. The first transition-programme seeds have been planted.
- **Crescendo, singleton, resource floor, diverse utopias.** Not started.

**Summary indicators**
- **Overall DU progress: 10/100 (+1).** Governance commitments were partly written into the IPO drafts and the jobs programme was seeded. These are offset by the legislative failure and the downgraded alignment evidence.
- **Catastrophe risk: Elevated, unchanged.** Defensive positioning for V5 and staged releases help. Measured eval-awareness and competitive release pressure (GPT-6, Gemini 4) offset them.
- **Public trust in AI: 35/100 (−1).** The DOJ-versus-New York fight and "flattering safety numbers" headlines feed a general unease.
- **Public trust in Anthropic: 50/100 (−1).** Credit for the candid correction is outweighed by the IPO-memo leak and the "regulatory capture" framing.
