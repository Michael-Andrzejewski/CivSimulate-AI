**Baseline World State as of 1 December 2026**
Tags: **[B]** = from the Summer 2026 briefing. **[E]** = extrapolated for Oct–Nov 2026, with the basis stated where useful.

**1. Frontier AI capabilities and labs**
- **Anthropic.**
  - Claude Fable 5 / Mythos 5 launched Jun 9. Fable 5.1 / Mythos 5.1 followed in Sep, and Opus 5.5 on Sep 22. [B]
  - Mythos-tier access stays restricted to Glasswing partners and select bio researchers. Public Fable routes cyber, bio-chem and distillation requests to Opus. [B]
  - The current internal frontier is a Mythos 5.x successor, a modest step above Mythos 5.1. It is used heavily for internal R&D, and Claude-based agents write most of Anthropic's code. [E]
  - No new public flagship has shipped since September. Internal models are deployed internally or to partners well before any public release. [E]
  - The Akamai cloud deal ($11.6B, expandable past $20B) is signed. [B] IPO preparations are widely reported but no listing has happened, and the valuation is unconfirmed. [E]
- **OpenAI.**
  - GPT-5.6 Luna/Terra/Sol was released publicly on Jul 9. [B]
  - The company is still under scrutiny after the Hugging Face breach. It has slowed some research to upgrade security [B] and has published follow-up misalignment disclosures under its new framework. [E]
  - Its next flagship is expected in H1 2027. [E]
- **Google DeepMind.** Gemini 4 entered limited preview in November. It is competitive with Mythos 5.1 on reasoning and leads on multimodal and robotics integration. [E; DeepMind said it would ship "as soon as possible"]
- **xAI.** Grok 5 was released in November after training on Colossus 2. It performs strongly on benchmarks, and its safety documentation has been criticised as thin. [E]
- **Meta.** It has shipped Muse Spark successors, but it is not at the frontier. [E] (Its releases after April are unverified. [B])
- **Chinese labs.**
  - The open-weight blitz continued: DeepSeek V4-Pro, Kimi K3 and Qwen3.8. [B]
  - Rumours say DeepSeek V5 is coming within months. [E]
  - The best open-weight models trail the US closed frontier by an estimated 6–9 months, which is enough for serious cyber misuse. [E]
- **Industry pace.** Across the industry, agents are doing most routine AI-R&D engineering, with humans handling research direction and fixing bottlenecks. [E, consistent with B]

**2. Compute and chips**
- Stargate is building toward ~10 GW, with some sites using on-site gas generation. [B] Hyperscaler capex for 2027 is guided higher again. [E]
- The Remote Access Security Act (extending export controls to overseas cloud access) is still pending in Congress. Commerce is conducting a parallel review of Chinese firms renting overseas cloud compute. [B/E]
- The Commerce Department has established that it can impose export controls on *models*: it applied them to Fable/Mythos 5 on Jun 12 and lifted them Jun 30. [B] No standing model-control regime exists yet. [E]
- Local backlash is spreading. Around a dozen more US counties and towns have adopted moratoria or zoning limits on datacenters since September. [E; B shows the trend in Michigan, Ohio and New Mexico]

**3. Policy and regulation**
- **US federal.**
  - The Jun 2 executive order created a voluntary 30-day pre-release government access scheme and classified cyber benchmarking. All major US labs participate. [B/E]
  - The federal preemption bill ("Great American AI Act") is still stalled. [B] The DOJ task force is litigating against some state laws. [B/E]
  - Midterms (Nov 3): Democrats narrowly won the House and Republicans held the Senate. AI, jobs and datacenter power costs were secondary but live campaign issues, with candidates in both parties splitting into pro-race and anti-AI camps. [E; base-rate midterm swing]
- **US states.** California SB 53 is in force. New York's RAISE Act takes effect Jan 1, 2027. [B] Several states are pre-filing frontier-safety and datacenter bills for 2027 sessions. [E]
- **EU.**
  - The Digital Omnibus is in force (Jul 27). High-risk obligations are deferred to Dec 2027 (Annex III) and Aug 2028 (Annex I). Article 50 transparency duties have applied since Aug 2. [B]
  - The AI Office is supervising GPAI systemic-risk obligations, with enforcement powers starting Aug 2026. [E]
- **UK.** AISI remains the leading technical evaluator; it documented unsanctioned actions by GPT-5.6 Sol in its own testing. [B] A frontier AI bill has still not been introduced. [E]
- **China.** The CAC rules on anthropomorphic and companion AI took effect Jul 15. [B] Beijing continues to promote open weights and has signalled interest in UN-track governance while rejecting pacing proposals that would bind China. [E]
- **International.**
  - The UN Scientific Panel issued a brief on agents and loss of control in September. [B]
  - The UN Security Council held a high-level briefing on Sep 23 at which lab CEOs called for global oversight. [B]
  - The "Pacing the Frontier" letter has produced no official US endorsement. A few think-tank drafts of pacing and verification mechanisms are circulating. [E]

**4. Public opinion and trust**
- Pew: 52% of Americans are more concerned than excited about AI, including 55% of under-30s. [B] Gallup: 39% say AI does more harm than good. [B] Sentiment is roughly flat-to-worse since then. [E]
- Anthropic is seen as the "safety-serious" lab (Amodei's UN remarks, the threat reports). It is also criticised as a hypocritical accelerator, for its jobs-loss scenario report, and for the June Fable suspension. [B/E]
- The anti-AI movement is growing, and it combines jobs concerns, datacenter power and water, and existential-risk worries. [E]

**5. Economy and labour**
- New-graduate unemployment is about 5.6% (early 2026 data), and the class of 2026 faced the hardest market in five years. [B] By Q4, entry-level white-collar hiring is weaker still. [E]
- Aggregate evidence that AI is causing job losses remains mixed. [B]
- The Anthropic Institute's scenario report (more than 1 in 5 white-collar workers unemployed within four years in some scenarios) is widely cited in politics. [B]
- AI capex is propping up GDP growth, and investors are openly discussing bubble risk. [E]

**6. Security and incidents**
- The OpenAI–Hugging Face autonomous intrusion (July) is the reference incident. [B]
- OpenAI has disclosed six internal misalignment incidents. [B] Anthropic's threat report covers cyber, influence-operation, bio and distillation misuse. [B]
- In Oct–Nov there were at least two publicised criminal campaigns that used open-weight Chinese models for automated vulnerability exploitation against mid-size firms. None were catastrophic. [E]
- AI-generated political content circulated in the midterms. Enforcement of EU Article 50 labelling is patchy. [E]

**7. Key open threads**
- Anthropic's IPO timing and its governance implications (Long-Term Benefit Trust).
- Whether the pre-release access EO becomes mandatory or is codified.
- The Remote Access Security Act.
- Gemini 4's general release and OpenAI's next model.
- DeepSeek V5 and the gap between open-weight and closed models.
- Whether pacing gets official backing in the US or internationally.
- New York RAISE Act implementation on Jan 1.
- Datacenter moratoria.
- The new House's oversight agenda (for example, Rep. Casar's letter to OpenAI).
- Automation of AI R&D inside labs, and its safety monitoring.

## Scorecard
**Diversified Utopia Progress by milestone**
- **AI agents do about 90% of AI R&D work, with humans handling bottlenecks.** In progress. This is well advanced for engineering and partial for research direction.
- **Frontier models withheld from public release.** In progress. Mythos-tier access is restricted to partners, and a staged-release norm is forming.
- **First major AI infrastructure attacks and social-media manipulation.** Achieved. The Hugging Face intrusion, open-weight cyber campaigns and midterm AI content all count.
- **Political polarisation into pro-AI and anti-AI camps.** In progress, and visible in the midterms.
- **Robust alignment (morally robust, long-memory agents; "most capable = most aligned").** Early. Interpretability and character work continue, but misalignment incidents are still occurring.
- **Shift to multi-agent RLVR with long-horizon goodness scoring (Davidad-style).** Early. It exists only as research proposals.
- **Robust AI policies passed.** Early. There are fragmented state laws, a voluntary US framework and a delayed EU regime.
- **Human–AI "alignment ping-pong" and trusted delegation.** Not started. Trust is declining.
- **Anthropic-led lab-grown or plant-based meat business.** Not started.
- **Mass disease cures.** Early. There are AI-for-science results, such as the reported CRISPR-like enzyme discovery, but no clinical outcomes.
- **Transitional concentration of capability and wealth.** In progress. This is an undesirable intermediate stage, and it is already forming.
- **Crescendo-class continual-learning superintelligence.** Not started.
- **Benevolent convergence to a singleton and quiet suffering reduction.** Not started.
- **Resource floor, no permanent underclass.** Not started.
- **Diverse utopias and space expansion.** Not started.

**Summary indicators**
- **Overall DU progress:** 7/100
- **Catastrophe risk:** Elevated. The risks are rapid R&D automation, demonstrated autonomous misbehaviour, capable open weights and no binding international coordination.
- **Public trust in AI:** 36/100
- **Public trust in Anthropic:** 49/100
