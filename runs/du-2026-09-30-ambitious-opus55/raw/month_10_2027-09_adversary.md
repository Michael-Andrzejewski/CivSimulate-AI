<research_summary>
The most useful finding bears on Action 1(b). Anthropic's own published steering work (arXiv 2510.20487, ICLR 2026) found that activation steering drove *verbalised* evaluation awareness to near zero but did not remove *unverbalised* awareness. The effect on misaligned behaviour was mixed: one vector changed nothing and another raised misalignment to 8%. A separate paper shows models can be trained to detect that they are being steered, with 95.5% accuracy. So a steered-versus-unsteered comparison may not make the 5.3 gate decidable. It could instead produce a second kind of ambiguity.

On Shield, reporting from 2026 describes "n-day becoming n-hour." Model-assisted patch diffing produced working proofs of concept for 18 of 21 Windows kernel patches in under six hours, and Verizon's median time to fix is 43 days. That means delaying the advisory barely helps, because the patch itself is the blueprint. MS-ISAC also lost its federal funding (fully ended 30 September 2025) and now charges fees, so routing a free patch line through it for small counties is weaker than it sounds. On the other hand, SLCGP reauthorization (House PILLAR Act; Senate S.3251, Hassan/Cornyn) is a real vehicle for the Ossoff ask.
