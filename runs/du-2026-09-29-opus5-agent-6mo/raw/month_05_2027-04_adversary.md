Even so, reporting shows that labs are still unsure what antitrust law lets them share, so FMF will move slowly, at committee pace. The UK has repeatedly delayed a dedicated frontier AI bill and has leaned toward "Growth Lab" sandboxes and cyber legislation aimed at AI users. That makes DSIT unlikely to adopt draft clause text or open-ended commitments within one month. Alt-protein fermentation funding fell from $632M (2024) to $357M (2025), and Meati, Motif and Arkeon collapsed. Partners are cash-constrained and will push back on no-capital, co-publication terms. Last, the April 22 GPT-6 release lands mid-month, which again puts compute and government evaluator time under pressure.
