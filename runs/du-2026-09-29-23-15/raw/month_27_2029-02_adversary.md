There is little new public evidence about the specific actors this month, so these threats extrapolate from current base rates. Research on hardware security keys and passkeys finds they cause real enrolment friction and lost-key lockouts, and adoption often stays in single digits after launch. That makes a hardware-key requirement on the verified bio lane likely to drive up over-refusal and appeals at exactly the point where the plan has a +0.5pp over-refusal cap. Opposition to datacenters has hardened: Gallup finds 71% of Americans oppose one near them, local moratoria are spreading, and New York imposed a statewide one-year moratorium. Unions such as IBEW, meanwhile, push labour standards rather than bans. Any "Good Neighbor Compute" deal with a moratorium town is therefore likely to be framed as buying off local democracy. In the world state, the partisan "incoming administration's lab" tag, the live Rakoff discovery, the 3–2 trustee vote and the virology set sitting at its line are all still in play. They make internal documents, CAISI closeness and two-sided safety criteria the most likely failure points.
