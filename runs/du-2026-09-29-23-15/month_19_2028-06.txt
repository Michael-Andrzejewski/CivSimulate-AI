Issues flagged:
- **Action 1 too generous for its margin.** A narrow partial success produced a clean pass on every condition, researcher re-engagement, a muted critic response and an 11% share jump. The only real "partial" elements are the rule's sunset clause and the virology subset.
- **Missing reactions to a full consumer rollout.** The apps went to full rollout within a week while the ladder-prompt family was still circulating. There were no red-teamer or jailbreak attempts on the apps, no press stress-testing and no misuse reports.
- **Unexplained gap change.** DeepSeek V5 moved from "2–3 months behind" to "1–2 months behind" with no stated cause.
- **Calendar details are loose.** The first Friday of June 2028 is June 2, not June 5. California's presidential primary is normally in March, not June; this was inherited from the prior state but should be corrected rather than propagated.
- **Hardship-based threat reductions.** Cutting threats by treating failed actions as covering the same harm is defensible, but halving the adversary's estimates across the board risks systematically under-weighting threats in future months.
