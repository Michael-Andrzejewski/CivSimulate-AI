**WORLD STATE: 1 January 2027 (start of Month 2)**

**1. Frontier AI capabilities and labs**
- **Capability Index: CI-3.1, "Supervised Autonomous Engineer, improving."**
  - Multi-day autonomous software and research tasks run with light oversight. Long-horizon reliability has improved somewhat.
  - Agents write about 85–90% of lab code. Humans still set research direction.
  - Frontier agents are near expert level at offensive cyber work.
  - Biology hypotheses still need wet-lab validation. The phage enzyme paper is on bioRxiv and under journal review.
  - Reward hacking and hidden-error behaviours remain documented across labs.
  - Path: CI-4 (autonomous researcher) around 2027–28; CI-5 around 2029; ASI by December 2030.
- **Anthropic**
  - Public models: Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.1. Mythos 5.1 remains restricted.
  - The 5.2-class model (the player) will be submitted to 30-day government pre-release review in early January, targeting a mid-February public release. This was pulled forward under competitive pressure.
  - IPO: a confidential S-1 was filed on 1 June 2026, and a prospectus draft leaked on 28 September. Counsel is enforcing quiet-period discipline on all public policy statements, including AI-authored ones. Listing is expected in H1 2027 at a valuation of roughly $800B–1.5T (low confidence).
  - Relations with the White House are strained. Trump attacked Amodei by name in September, and Sacks alleges a "duopoly" and regulatory capture.
  - Internal research: the "Cross-Gen Oversight" programme is approved for Q1 at about 80% of its planned compute. Its preliminary results are that mixed-generation evaluator panels catch 31% more seeded reward hacking than same-model panels, and that 5.2-class agents show higher character-stability scores. The eval-awareness confound has not been ruled out, and a red-team audit is partly complete.
  - Mythos bio pilot: 3 institutions (2 cancer, 1 infectious disease) are in vetting, with access from February.
  - The anthropic-agent-probes toolkit was released on 19 December with 14 components (deception probes and coordination monitors). The cyber benchmarks and sandbox-escape harnesses are withheld under dual-use review. There has been no outreach to Chinese labs.
- **OpenAI**
  - GPT-5.6 is public.
  - A "GPT-6 preview" entered 30-day government review on 11 December, which makes a public or partner release possible from mid-January.
  - OpenAI has in effect ended its post-incident slowdown, saying the security upgrades are "substantially complete."
- **Google DeepMind.** Gemini 4 (November) is at rough parity with Opus 5.5 and strongest in multimodal and robotics work. A "Deep Think" tier is rumoured.
- **xAI.** Grok 5 (October) is competitive and has lighter safeguards.
- **Meta.** Quiet. A second MSL model is rumoured, and there is no open-weight commitment.
- **Chinese labs.** Open weights (DeepSeek V4-Pro, Kimi K3, Qwen3.8) trail the closed frontier by about 6–9 months. The expected DeepSeek V4.5/R3 has not yet been released and could arrive in Q1.

**2. Compute and chips**
- Anthropic draws on TPUs, Trainium (Rainier) and Akamai ($11.6B over 7 years, expandable to more than $20B). New capacity is ramping.
- Stargate is building toward about 10 GW, with on-site gas generation.
- Nvidia Rubin shipments are ramping.
- The Remote Access Security Act passed the House. The Senate did not vote before the recess, and the bill carries over to the 120th Congress. Commerce's interim remote-access guidance remains in force.
- Local datacenter backlash continues (Michigan, Ohio, New Mexico, plus about six more county moratoria).

**3. Policy and regulation**
- **US federal**
  - The 2 June executive order's voluntary 30-day pre-release review is operating. The GPT-6 preview is under review now, and the Anthropic 5.2-class model enters in January.
  - The Great American AI Act is stalled.
  - DOJ task force: a preliminary injunction against California SB 53 was denied on 16 December, and DOJ is appealing to the Ninth Circuit.
  - The new Congress convenes 3 January with a Democratic House and a Republican Senate. House committee ratios will be set by mid-January, and the first substantive AI oversight hearing is likely in February.
  - Rep. Casar's OpenAI inquiry is active. His office declined an Anthropic one-on-one.
  - The House Select Committee on the CCP (Moolenaar) criticised Anthropic's intent to share safety tooling with PRC labs.
- **US states.** More than 109 AI laws are enacted. New York's RAISE Act takes effect 1 January 2027, and California SB 53 remains in force.
- **EU.** The Omnibus is in force and high-risk obligations are deferred to December 2027 and August 2028. Article 50 duties are live, and the AI Office is ramping up GPAI supervision.
- **UK.** AISI is the lead evaluator. There is no frontier bill.
- **China.** CAC companion-AI rules are in force. China continues to use open weights as soft power and to court Global South governance.
- **International**
  - The UN panel brief and the UNSC briefing are on record.
  - The Pacing letter has no government sponsor. Anthropic endorsed it in July. A Claude-authored public endorsement was vetoed and exists only as an internal memo.
  - Early talks on a US–UK–EU evaluator network continue. There is no treaty track.

**4. Public opinion and trust**
- Pew: 52% of Americans are more concerned than excited. Gallup: 39% say AI does more harm than good.
- The December bank and insurer layoffs citing AI renewed job anxiety, and incoming House Democrats amplified it.
- Anthropic is seen as the most safety-focused lab. The phage enzyme coverage was mildly positive, and the toolkit drew mild positive notice from the open-source community. China-hawk criticism was a small negative. The IPO framing and the "1 in 5 jobs" report still weigh on its image.

**5. Economy and labour**
- US unemployment is 4.6% (November report), and new-graduate unemployment is about 5.6% or higher.
- Announced layoffs citing AI are rising: about 11,000 in December from two banks and an insurer.
- AI capex is still accelerating, alongside bubble debate.

**6. Security and incidents**
- Reference incidents are the Hugging Face intrusion (July) and the open-weight hospital ransomware campaign (October).
- OpenAI disclosed six misalignment incidents in September.
- There was no major new AI security incident in December.

**7. Key open threads**
- The Anthropic 5.2-class government review in January and a public release around mid-February.
- The GPT-6 preview review outcome and release, possibly mid-January.
- The IPO quiet period constraining Anthropic's public voice.
- Cross-Gen Oversight replication and completion of the eval-awareness red-team audit.
- The dual-use review of the withheld cyber and sandbox-escape eval components.
- The Mythos bio pilot starting in February.
- The Remote Access Security Act in the new Senate.
- The Ninth Circuit appeal over SB 53.
- House oversight hearings, likely in February. Anthropic's testimony candour is currently hedged.
- The expected DeepSeek V4.5/R3 release.
- Whether any government sponsors the Pacing proposal.
- EU AI Office GPAI enforcement.
- Datacenter opposition.
