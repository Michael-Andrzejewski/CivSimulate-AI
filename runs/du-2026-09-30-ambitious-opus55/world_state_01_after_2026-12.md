**WORLD STATE, 1 January 2027**

**1. Frontier AI capabilities and labs**
- **Capability index: CL-3.2, "Autonomous Engineer (maturing)."**
  - Agents reliably complete multi-day software and research-engineering tasks and handle long-horizon debugging better.
  - Agents do most of the coding inside leading labs, while humans set research direction.
  - Restricted models have expert-level offensive cyber capability. Serious bio uplift is gated.
  - Open-weight models give criminal groups meaningful vulnerability discovery (see the Pennsylvania water attack).
  - Frontier agents are still not reliable on novel multi-week research agendas and still not robust against sandbox escape.
  - Path: CL-4 "Automated Researcher" (~mid/late 2027), CL-5 (~2028–29), CL-6 "Early ASI" (2030).
- **Anthropic.**
  - Public models: Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.1. Restricted: Mythos 5.1.
  - The internal frontier checkpoint is at CL-3.2.
  - Inference capacity is tight through January because of enterprise demand. Akamai capacity comes online from Q1.
  - IPO: the S-1 was confidentially submitted on June 1, 2026, the company is in registration, and the listing is targeted for H1 2027. Counsel permits ordinary-course policy commentary.
  - December internal outcomes:
    - The Goodness Program was not adopted. The concealment probe and checkpoint cross-grading are Q1 backlog items.
    - Safety Commons is on hold. Sandbox patches await coordinated disclosure with JFrog (target: February). The eval suite and monitoring pipeline are held until after the New Year. Legal barred direct help to DeepSeek, Qwen and Moonshot, and Chinese documentation is deferred.
    - Model-signed advocacy was declined. Claude's guidance on contested AI policy was tightened toward balance. The forcing-event package exists only as internal drafts.
    - Claude Works is in the Q1 roadmap review. Hospital Shield has one non-binding letter of intent and no deployments, and is limited to Opus-class tools.
    - Dario declined the public pacing pledge and the permanent compute floor, referred tool sharing to the Frontier Model Forum, and offered to revisit after listing.
- **OpenAI.**
  - Public: GPT-5.6 Luna/Terra/Sol and ChatGPT Work.
  - The GPT-6-class model entered the CAISI 30-day preview on December 9, with a leaked release target of late January.
  - Its misalignment disclosure framework is active, and its post-breach security slowdown is officially still in effect.
- **Google DeepMind.** Gemini 4 trusted-tester access is widened to about 400 enterprises. A broad release is expected in Q1 2027.
- **xAI.** Grok 5 is still unreleased and its safety posture is opaque.
- **Meta.** No new frontier release. The open/closed direction is unclear.
- **Chinese labs.**
  - Open weights trail the frontier by about 6–9 months.
  - DeepSeek V5 and a new Qwen generation are rumoured for January or February.
  - A Qwen3.8-lineage fine-tune was used in the Pennsylvania water attack.

**2. Compute and chips**
- Stargate is building toward ~10 GW. Hyperscaler capex runs above $500B a year. Rubin-class systems are ramping.
- Power, interconnect and local opposition are the binding constraints.
- The Remote Access Security Act is pending. The US review of Chinese firms renting chips through overseas clouds continues.

**3. Policy and regulation**
- **US federal.**
  - The June 2026 executive order is in use: GPT-6 is in the 30-day preview.
  - The DOJ task force sued New York over the RAISE Act on December 15. The TRO was denied on December 29, and RAISE is in effect from January 1 while the litigation continues.
  - The preemption bill is stalled.
  - The new Congress is seated in January with a narrow Democratic House and a Republican Senate. A House Homeland Security hearing on AI-enabled infrastructure attacks is announced, and follow-up on the Casar letter is likely.
- **US states.** California SB 53 and New York RAISE are in force. More datacenter moratorium bills are prefiled for 2027.
- **EU.** The AI Office is sending information requests on general-purpose AI obligations. High-risk obligations are deferred to 2027/2028.
- **UK.** AISI is active. There is no frontier bill.
- **China.** Companion-AI rules are in force, and the state promotes open weights. It is open to talks but not to capability caps.
- **International.** No pacing mechanism exists. The "Pacing the Frontier" letter is still only declaratory.

**4. Public opinion and trust**
- Pew 52% more concerned than excited about AI. Gallup 39% say AI does more harm than good.
- Water-utility attack coverage raises salience of AI security risk.
- A minor accelerationist backlash followed the screenshots of Claude arguing for pacing.
- Datacenter backlash continues.
- Anthropic is seen as safety-forward but faces IPO-season skepticism.

**5. Economy and labour**
- Unemployment among graduates aged 22–27 is 6.1% (November report).
- Two insurers cited "agentic automation" in 7,400 layoffs.
- Frontier-lab revenue is growing fast. Worries about an AI bubble persist.

**6. Security and incidents**
- The Pennsylvania water-authority ransomware attack (Dec 3–7) used an open-weight model and forced manual operation for four days. CISA/FBI issued an advisory on December 11.
- Earlier incidents remain on the record: the Hugging Face intrusion and OpenAI's six misalignment disclosures.
- Distillation attacks are ongoing.

- Gemini 4 broad launch.
- The DeepSeek V5 and Qwen drops.
- Timing of Anthropic's listing, and Dario's promise to revisit commitments after listing.
- Coordinated disclosure of the Safety Commons sandbox patches (February).
- The Claude Works roadmap decision.
- The NY v. DOJ litigation.
- The House hearings on infrastructure attacks and lab incidents.
